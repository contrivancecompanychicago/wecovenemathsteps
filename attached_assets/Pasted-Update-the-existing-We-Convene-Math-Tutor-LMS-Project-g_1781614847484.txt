CRA means:

Stage 1: Concrete should help students understand mathematics through hands-on objects, manipulatives, visual grouping, measuring, sorting, building, and explaining before using formulas or symbolic procedures.

3. Create Concrete Lesson Template

Use this structure for every Stage 1 lesson:

Explore:
Student uses physical objects or manipulatives.

Observe:
Student describes what changed, what stayed the same, and what they notice.

Explain:
Student explains the mathematical relationship in words.

Connect:
Student connects the physical model to a drawing, table, number line, or diagram.

Symbolize:
Only after understanding, student writes the equation, expression, or formula.

Reflect:
Student explains what they learned and what still feels confusing.
