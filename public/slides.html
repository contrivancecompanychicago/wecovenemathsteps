Learning Framework
Concrete → Representational → Abstract
A research-backed instructional progression that builds deep understanding before symbols.
01
Stage 1
Concrete
Students use physical objects, manipulatives, and hands-on exploration to build intuition before any formula is introduced.
02
Stage 2
Representational
Students draw diagrams, tables, number lines, and visual models that bridge the physical to the symbolic.
03
Stage 3
Abstract
Only after true understanding, students write equations, expressions, and formal procedures with confidence.
Full path: Concrete → Representational → Abstract → Transfer → Modeling → Mathematical Independence
